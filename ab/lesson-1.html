Lab: Build “Instantaneous” from “Average”

You’ll shrink the interval and watch the average rate “settle” toward a number.

Synthesis: The Big Idea You Earned

Now we name the thing you built by experimenting.
Planned: 8 min
You discovered:
  • Average rate uses \\(\\frac{\\Delta s}{\\Delta t}\\) and needs an interval.
  • At a point, \\(\\Delta t=0\\) causes division-by-zero trouble, so average rate at a single instant is undefined. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}
  • The limit concept defines the instantaneous rate as the value that the average rates approach as intervals shrink. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

Vocabulary (student-friendly)

  • Average rate of change: “change per unit input over an interval.”
  • Instantaneous rate: “what average rates approach as the interval shrinks.”
  • Representation: the same idea shown by table, graph, equation, or words.
