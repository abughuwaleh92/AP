Now We Name the Idea: “Limit”

Informal definition: \[ \lim_{x\to a} f(x)=L \] means: as \(x\) gets close to \(a\) (not necessarily equal), \(f(x)\) gets close to \(L\).

Two rules that save lives

  • Limits care about near \(a\), not necessarily at \(a\).
  • A two-sided limit exists only if left limit = right limit.
Translation: if left and right disagree, the limit is DNE.

Tap: formal definition (ε–δ)

\[ \lim_{x\to a} f(x)=L \text{ means: for every }\varepsilon>0\text{ there exists }\delta>0 \] such that if \(0<|x-a|<\delta\), then \(|f(x)-L|<\varepsilon\).
You don’t need full mastery yet — but we’ll make it feel real with a sandbox later.
