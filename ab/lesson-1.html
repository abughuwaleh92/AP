Tap to Reveal — Limit vs Value

Consider a function that has a hole at \(x=2\), but the graph approaches \(y=5\).

Statements
Key idea

Limit: what \(f(x)\) is heading to as \(x\) approaches \(2\).

Value: what happens exactly at \(x=2\).

If the hole is at \((2,5)\), then the limit can be 5 even if \(f(2)\) is undefined.
